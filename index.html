OUR STORY

We grew up watching our grandmothers tear a bay leaf into a pot and know instantly, by smell alone, whether it was any good.

Most spice on shelves today isn't. It's been sitting in a warehouse for a year, dried too fast, stored too loosely, and it shows — flat, dusty, barely there. Pristine Naturals exists to fix that one leaf at a time.

We work directly with small farms across India, sun-dry every batch slowly, and pack it ourselves in small runs — so what reaches your kitchen still smells like it just came off the tree.

HOW IT'S MADE

Farm to pouch,
in four steps.

No middle-man warehouses, no bulk imports sitting in customs for months. Just a short, deliberate chain from farm to your kitchen shelf.

01

Handpicked

Leaves and bark are selected by hand at peak oil content, not machine-stripped off the branch.

02

Sun-dried

Slow, shaded drying over several days preserves the natural oils that give real aroma.

03

Hand-sorted

Every batch is checked leaf by leaf for colour, size and quality before it's cleared to pack.

04

Sealed fresh

Packed in resealable pouches within days, batch-coded for full traceability back to source.
